    Our Milestones

    2002: Founded

    Mipet Services Limited was established to provide reliable and innovative solutions in the Oil & Gas and Infastructure Sectors

    2005: Establishment of Western/Eastern Regional offices -

    Mipet Services Limited Western/Eastern offices were established, extending service coverage and client support accross Nigeria.

    2010: Global Expansion

    Expanded operations through strategic international partnerships, strenghtening technical capacity and aligning with global best practices.

    2012: Strengthened Regulatory and Quality Compliance.

    Attained key industry certifications and regulatory approvals, comprising NUPRC Permit, NMDPRA Permit, Nogic Certificate, NSITF Compliance and ISO 9001:2015 Quality Managment System Certification - Reaffirming our Commitment to quality, safety, and operational excellence.

    2013: First Major Execution:

    Delivered the Company's first full trailer-mounted acidization package, a landmark achievement demonstrating engineering excellence in oilfield operations.

    2015: Partnership With Key OEMs

    Partnered with Original Equipment Manafacturers (OEMs) to advance technology transfer and support Nigeria's Local Content aspirations.

    2017: Establishment of Mipet Services Limited Engineering & Technical Team

    Formed a specialized Engineering and Technical Division to deliver integrated solutions accross Oil & gas and Infractructural Projects.

    2023/24: Mipet Mud Laboratory and Mud Plant was Established

    Established a state-of-the-art Mud Laboratory and fully equiped Mud Plant, strenghtening and enhancing our drilling fluid analysis, drilling engineering, drilling fluid management services and capabilities to better support clients in exploration and production.
