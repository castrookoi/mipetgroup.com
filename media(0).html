    Our Milestones

    2002: Company Founded

    Launched MiPet Services, embarking on a mission to deliver innovative energy solutions.

    2004: First Pipeline Project

    Completed our inaugural pipeline installation, establishing our midstream expertise.

    2007: Offshore Drilling Success

    Achieved a breakthrough in offshore drilling operations, enhancing our upstream capabilities.

    2010: International Expansion

    Expanded our services to global markets, serving clients across multiple continents.

    2013: Technology Integration

    Adopted advanced automation technologies, boosting operational efficiency.

    2015: Community Initiative Launch

    Initiated community support programs, enhancing local infrastructure and welfare.

    2017: Major Refinery Upgrade

    Led a significant refinery modernization project, optimizing downstream operations.

    2019: Sustainability Milestone

    Implemented eco-friendly practices, reducing environmental impact in our projects.

    2020: Safety Excellence Award

    Recognized for outstanding safety standards across all operational sectors.

    2022: 20th Anniversary

    Celebrated two decades of delivering excellence in energy and infrastructure.

    2023: Renewable Energy Venture

    Entered the renewable energy sector with innovative solar and wind solutions.

    2025: Digital Transformation

    Rolled out a digital platform to enhance project management and client engagement.
