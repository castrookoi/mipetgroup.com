    Our Milestones

    2002: Founded

    Established MiPet Services Limited, beginning our journey in delivering oil, gas, and infrastructure solutions.

    2005: First Major Project

    Completed our first significant oil and gas project, setting the foundation for future growth.

    2010: Global Expansion

    Expanded operations to serve international clients, marking our presence in global markets.

    2012: Technology Adoption

    Integrated advanced technologies into our operations, enhancing efficiency and service quality.

    2015: 100th Project

    Successfully completed our 100th project, showcasing our expertise and reliability.
